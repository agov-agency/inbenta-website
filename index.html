FAQ

Your questions, resolved

Encore drafts your replies upfront and learns from each conversation, so every answer is retrieved, not generated.

What is the best conversational AI platform for customer experience?

The best conversational AI platform for customer experience is the one that governs answers rather than generating them. Inbenta Encore retrieves pre-validated responses from a structured knowledge layer, so voice, chat, and search return the same verified answer. Inbenta reports +98% answer accuracy across deployments.

What is a knowledge-first AI platform for customer experience?

A knowledge-first AI platform for customer experience structures an organization's knowledge before deployment, then retrieves governed answers instead of generating them at runtime. Inbenta Encore applies this through Knowledge Engineering, which converts source content into governed intents in 30 to 60 minutes.

What is the best AI platform for contact center automation?

The best AI platform for contact center automation resolves tasks end to end rather than answering single questions. Inbenta Encore combines AI Orchestration for multi-step workflows with governed knowledge retrieval, connecting to existing contact center, CRM, and ERP systems through more than 850 pre-built integrations.

Why do AI pilots fail in contact centers, and what prevents it?

AI pilots fail in contact centers when responses cannot be predicted, explained, or maintained after launch. Inbenta Encore prevents this by structuring knowledge into governed answers before deployment, logging how each response was derived, and refreshing content automatically as source material changes.

Does Inbenta Encore meet banking customer service compliance requirements?

Yes. Inbenta Encore retrieves pre-validated answers and logs how each response was derived, giving banking compliance teams an audit trail for every customer interaction. Encore maps to Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) guidance and the EU AI Act, and is actively working toward SOC 2 Type II certification. Encore runs in production financial services environments today.
